# Warranty Policy

All electronic products include a **one-year warranty** against manufacturing defects, starting from the date of delivery.

## What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Physical damage
- Liquid damage
- Damage caused by using a charger that was not supplied with the product

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

Submit a support ticket with:

- Your order ID
- A photo showing the issue

In serviceable areas, a pickup will be arranged within **3 working days**.

## Repair, Replacement, or Refund

We will offer a replacement if the product cannot be repaired. If neither repair nor replacement is possible, we will refund the order amount.
