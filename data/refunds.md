# Refund Policy

You may request a refund within **7 days of delivery**. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Refund Processing

Once we receive the returned item, the refund will be processed within **5 to 7 working days**. The money will be returned to the original payment method. UPI refunds are usually completed faster than card refunds.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following items cannot be refunded:

- Opened software
- Gift cards
- Items marked as final sale

## Cancelling an Unshipped Order

If your order has not yet shipped, cancellation is faster than requesting a refund because the amount is released on the same day.
