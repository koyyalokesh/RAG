# Shipping and Delivery

## Order Processing

Orders placed before **2:00 PM** are packed on the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM are packed on the next working day.

## Estimated Delivery Times

- **Metro cities:** 2 working days
- **Other cities:** 4 to 5 working days
- **Remote PIN codes:** Up to 8 working days

## Delivery Charges

- Orders above **₹999** qualify for free delivery.
- Orders of **₹999 or less** incur a flat delivery fee of **₹49**, which is added at checkout.

## Order Tracking

A tracking link will be sent by email and SMS as soon as the courier picks up your parcel.
